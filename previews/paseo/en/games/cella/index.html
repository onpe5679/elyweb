Synopsis & Story

STORY ARCHIVE

One year has passed since the war ended. Fed on the ashes of conflict, the city grew faster than ever—yet its people were left behind. This is Actain, a factory metropolis drowned in the roar of massive steam engines and grinding gears.

Behind the blinding light of reconstruction lie buried scars of war and pasts erased in the name of advancement. Where uncomfortable truths run rampant, the police offload dangerous cases onto private investigators under the guise of "cooperation."

Amidst this turbulent era, Ciella—a former police officer and war veteran—enters into a dangerous alliance to uncover the truth behind her father’s unsolved death.

Steampunk Noir in the Factory Metropolis of Actain
