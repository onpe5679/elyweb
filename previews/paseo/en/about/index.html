For Story-Lovers, By Storytellers

Opening New Horizons of Interactive Literature — Studio Elysian

Founded in 2022, Studio Elysian Inc. creates story-driven games uniting scenario writing, illustration, animation, and original music. Alongside our war-themed "Old Engine" literary cycle, we operate subculture romance label "Studio Habom" and animation studio "Elysian Pictures".
