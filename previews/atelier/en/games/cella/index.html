Ciella's Casebook

War is over... Or maybe not.

01

Synopsis

A year has passed since the war ended. The city, fed on the ashes of conflict, grew faster than ever—but its people were left behind. This is 'Actain,' a factory city drowned in the roar of massive steam engines and grinding gears. Behind the blinding light of reconstruction and progress lie the buried scars of war, and pasts erased in the name of advancement. In a place where uncomfortable truths run rampant, the police are shifting their burdens to private investigators under the guise of "co-operation." Amidst this turbulent era, Ciella—a former cop and war veteran— enters into a dangerous alliance to uncover the truth behind her father’s cold case...
