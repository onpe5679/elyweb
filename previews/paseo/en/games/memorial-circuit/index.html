Synopsis & Story

STORY ARCHIVE

The devastating four-year war that swept the continent has ended. Yet for genius engineer Lukas, all that remains is the empty space left by his wife Hana, an army medical officer who never returned.

Confined in grief, Lukas receives an eerie offer from the head of Plot Industry, a megacorporation leading national engineering projects: "What if you could bring your dead wife back?"

With nothing left to lose, Lukas accepts. Hana awakens inside a cold mechanical body—but with all her memories gone. As Lukas digs deeper to restore their ordinary days, the sinister truths behind Plot Industry and the war surface.

Key Highlights

01

Prologue to the Old Engine Universe · Sci-Fi Romance Thriller

The first entry in the "Old Engine" anthology exploring war and its scars. Experience a dense 4–6 hour narrative with a core storyline and branching bad/side endings.

02

Motion Graphics & Comic-Panel Direction

Going beyond static text boxes, Memorial Circuit integrates video motion graphics and comic-strip panel layouts to heighten dramatic tension and immersion.

03

Full 4K Visuals, Voice Acting & Vocal OST

Presented in crisp Full 4K resolution with dedicated Korean voice acting and original vocal themes ("MEMORIAL RECIPE" and "MEMORIAL THINGS").
