Dialogue & Choices

Tap through the lines. Make the choices yourself.

A short taste of Habom's mood. Step through the dialogue one line at a time, pick your answers, and see where the story goes.

  1. 1

    Move through the lines

    Press while the text is appearing to show it all at once, and press again to go to the next line.

  2. 2

    Pick your choices

    Click or tap, or use the 1 · 2 · 3 keys. There are three choice points.

  3. 3

    Reach an ending

    Your choices lead to different endings. The ending card recaps what you picked, and you can play again.
