AUTHOR’S NOTE · 창작 선언문FOLIO 2022.09 – PRESENT

“We write sentences that stay with you long after the final page is turned.”

Founded in 2022, Studio Elysian is a narrative game studio uniting literary scenario writing, expressive illustration, original soundtracks, and animation direction.

Through our "Old Engine" series ("Memorial Circuit", "The Festival Isn’t Over", "Cella") we examine war, memory, and human resilience, while expanding into cosmic fantasy ("In Between All Times") and bright romance comedy ("Welcome to Sharehouse!").
