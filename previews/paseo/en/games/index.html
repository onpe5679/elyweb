Memorial Circuit

After a four-year war, a scientist revives his lost wife in a mechanical body—only to confront the abyss of her erased memories.

“What if you could bring your late wife back to life? If you cannot remember, doubt everything.”

The devastating four-year war that swept the continent has ended. Yet for genius engineer Lukas, all that remains is the empty space left by his wife Hana, an army medical officer who never returned.

In Between All Times

On a campus drifting through deep space, a graduating senior and a mysterious clubroom ghost spend one final year writing a short story together.

“What can we do in the time between? If there were one thing you wished to finish before graduation, what would it be?”

In a surreal, romantic cosmos where humanity lives among the stars without spacesuits, a fourth-year sociology student approaches graduation unable to decide on their future.

The Festival Isn't Over

As a town prepares for a joyous New Year festival, retired soldier Isha traces the memory threads of a three-year war across seven haunting days.

“Some wounds never fully mend, and some memories remain no matter how hard you try to forget.”

Though the brutal three-year war has ended and public memory is fading, retired soldier Isha remains trapped in the shadows of the past as each day blurs into the next.

Ciella's Casebook

In Actain, a factory metropolis drowned in the roar of steam engines, former war veteran and detective Ciella forms a dangerous alliance to solve her father’s cold case.

“The war is over... or perhaps it never truly ended. Beneath the banner of progress, erased pasts still breathe in the steam.”

One year has passed since the war ended. Fed on the ashes of conflict, the city grew faster than ever—yet its people were left behind. This is Actain, a factory metropolis drowned in the roar of massive steam engines and grinding gears.
