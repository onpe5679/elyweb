Synopsis & Story

STORY ARCHIVE

In the year 20XX, after failing college entrance exams, you spend your days as an unemployed homebody watching anime in your room—until a smack on the back from your parents sends you packing to Seoul.

Told to "live with hardworking people and learn from them," you move into a shared house in Seoul. Yet the moment you open the front door, you discover your three housemates are all stunning girls!

From energetic literature student Ryu Jihye, to cool-yet-clumsy startup worker Park Yunha, to aloof freelance composer Hong Seeun—can you figure out your future and find love under one roof?

Key Highlights

01

Our Story · Relatable 20s Cohabitation Romance Comedy

Turning the real-life worries of twenty-somethings into warm, laugh-out-loud comedy as you navigate work, dreams, and romance alongside three unforgettable roommates.

02

High-Detail Character Illustrations & Expressive Animation

Crafted with meticulous attention to each heroine’s charm points, expressive sprites, and vibrant event CGs that make every conversation sparkle.

03

Intuitive UI, Full Voice Acting & 3 Original Vocal OSTs

Designed with a clean, effortless interface so you can focus purely on the romance—complete with full Korean voice acting and three original vocal themes.
